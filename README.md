# medlingo

medlingo is a language-learning tool for clinicians, built around medical phrases for speaking with patients in their own language. It is for students and clinicians who speak English or do not know the exact clinical terminology in another language.

Pick a language, browse clinical areas, or search in English, translation or pronunciation. Each phrase includes an English-sound respelling for pronunciation. Open any card in Study view for large-text practice, or use the Quiz tab to test recall across specialties and directions.

There are 2,142 phrases across 14 specialties in 10 languages: Arabic, Cantonese, Farsi, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Punjabi, Tagalog and Vietnamese. Translations with a Reviewed tag have been checked by a bilingual reviewer, and translations without a reviewer are drafts awaiting bilingual review. medlingo also keeps the Medical Questionnaire (12,602 phrases) by Bouillon, Gerlach, Mutal, Tsourakis and Spechbach (CC BY 4.0) as a backup when a search finds no medlingo match.
